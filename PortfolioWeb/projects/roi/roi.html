Roi ✦ ✧ ✦

Software Construction

What ?

"Roi“ is a 2-4 player cards game. Goal of the game : It challenges the players’ memory. They must remember their own cards as well as the other players’ cards. Players can take advantage of special powers that certain cards provide. The objective is to collect low-value cards, call out “Roi”, and have the lowest total score to win.

How ?

Developed in Scala by a team of four. Collaborated through regular meetings to design the game concept and define its rules Implementation of the backend, User interface design and testing of this multiplayers game.
