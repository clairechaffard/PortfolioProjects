Pratiques artistiques - interactivité

What ?

A video game, walking simulator developed by a team of 3 Players explore different stages of a chracter’s life after an accident leaves them with fragmented memories. Through exploration and environmental storytelling, playres gradually reconstruct the character’s past and uncover the circumstances of the accident. A dreamlike journey through memory, loss and self-discovery.

How ?

Developed with Unity after a prototype phase. Designed a environmental storytelling player’s journey. Created interactive scripts, including an alternative ending based on the player’s gameplay. Integrated and adapted assets to build immersive environments. Explored rendering techniques including a dotted effect to enhance the game’s dreamy atmosphere.
