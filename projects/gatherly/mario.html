Mario Kart

Computer Graphics

What ?

Mario-Kart inspired 3D scene featuring animation and characters Users can choose effects they want to apply to the scene Several real-time effects are available, including toon shading, depth of field and SSAO.

How ?

The scene is rendered in WebGL using Regl, JavaScript and GLSL. Animations are calculated along Bezier curve paths The 3d was conceptualized using reference materials from public libraries.
